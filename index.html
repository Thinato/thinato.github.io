WELCOME TO MY HOMEPAGE

Hello there cyber-traveler! 👋 My name is PAULO and you have stumbled upon my little corner of the World Wide Web!! I hail from the magical land of CURITIBA, BRAZIL 🇧🇷 — the city of pinheiros and cold rain. Make yourself at home, grab a virtual cafezinho, and DON'T FORGET TO SIGN MY GUESTBOOK!!!

WHAT I DO ALL DAY

I am a SOFTWARE ENGINEER at a super cool company called BRICK 🧱 where we build AI agent infrastructure and workflow automation. Imagine telling somebody in 1998 that one day you'd dispatch autonomous robots to write your TypeScript for you. They would call you a wizard. 🧙

My weapons of choice: Helix editor (with custom keybinds, of course), a beautiful monorepo, pnpm + Turborepo, Zod schemas everywhere, and an unhealthy obsession with atomic PRs.
